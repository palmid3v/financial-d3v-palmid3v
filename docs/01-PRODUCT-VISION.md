# Product Vision

Financial-D3v is a private personal-finance application and financial learning workspace.

It is a real financial product for its owner while also serving as a practical engineering project for learning Go, backend development, software architecture, databases, testing and disciplined AI-assisted development.

## Core promise

Help the user answer:
- Where is my money?
- Where does my money go?
- What can I safely spend?
- What am I saving for?
- How am I progressing toward my goals?
- What debts and obligations do I have?
- What do I own and what do I owe?
- How is my net worth changing?
- What financial concept should I understand better?
- What habit or decision should I review next?

## Product pillars

### 1. Financial control
Accounts, transactions, income, expenses, categories, budgets, savings goals, debts, assets, liabilities and net worth.

### 2. Financial education
The application turns the user's own financial activity into learning opportunities. It can explain budgets, savings progress, debt balances, net worth, cash flow and spending patterns.

### 3. Long-term financial improvement
The product should help the user develop better financial awareness and saving habits through measurement, reflection and clear feedback.

### 4. Engineering education
Financial-D3v is also a practical Go project. The user learns software engineering concepts by implementing a real system with meaningful domain rules.

## Payroll boundary

Payroll remains a specialized domain, but it is not the product's identity. The personal-finance core comes first.

## Product principles

1. Private by design.
2. User-owned financial data.
3. Clarity over complexity.
4. Deterministic financial calculations.
5. Education in context.
6. Traceable recommendations and explanations.
7. No invented legal or tax rules.
8. Small, validated releases.
9. The financial ledger is the source of truth.
10. The UI explains; the domain decides.
