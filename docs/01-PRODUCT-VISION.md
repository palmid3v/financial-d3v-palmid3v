# Product Vision

Financial-D3v is a Go-first financial workspace for understanding and managing money, obligations, wealth and payroll.

## Core promise

Provide one coherent model for money in and out, accounts and transactions, planning, savings, debts and liabilities, assets and net worth, reporting, and payroll.

The product must remain traceable and deterministic rather than becoming a collection of unrelated CRUD screens.

## Product boundary

Payroll is a specialized financial domain. It is not the entire product.

## Design priorities

1. Clarity.
2. Deterministic calculations.
3. Traceability.
4. Secure ownership boundaries.
5. Incremental delivery.
