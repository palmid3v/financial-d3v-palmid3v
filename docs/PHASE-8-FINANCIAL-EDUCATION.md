# FASE 8 — Financial Education\n\n**Status: APPROVED / BUILT**\n\nFASE 8 adds financial education as a first-class backend capability. The product does not merely display numbers; it explains what a number means, how it is calculated, what it may indicate, and what the owner can review next.\n\n## Education model\n\n**FACT → CALCULATION → INTERPRETATION → ACTION**\n\n- **FACT** — describes the financial concept without judgment.\n- **CALCULATION** — states the formula or aggregation used.\n- **INTERPRETATION** — explains what the calculated result can and cannot indicate.\n- **ACTION** — gives a concrete review or planning step.\n\nInterpretations are deliberately not presented as guaranteed facts about the user's financial health.\n\n## Initial topics\n\n- Cash flow\n- Budget utilization\n- Savings progress\n- Transaction categorization\n- Debt basics\n\nDebt education is conceptual in FASE 8. Debt balances, payments, interest and fees remain FASE 9.\n\n## API\n\n### Education catalog\n\n`GET /api/v1/education`\n\nOptional filter:\n\n`GET /api/v1/education?topic=budget`\n\nReturns educational cards without requiring financial data.\n\n### Contextual education\n\n`GET /api/v1/education/insights`\n\nOptional query parameters:\n\n- `budgetId`\n- `goalId`\n\nWhen identifiers are supplied, the service uses the authoritative budget and savings services to attach the relevant metric context to the educational cards.\n\n## Boundaries\n\nFASE 8 does not:\n\n- invent financial regulations or tax rules;\n- provide guaranteed financial outcomes;\n- execute financial operations;\n- replace accounting, tax or legal advice;\n- create debt accounting before FASE 9;\n- duplicate budget or savings calculations.\n\n## Example learning loop\n\n1. The owner records transactions.\n2. A budget aggregates actual expenses.\n3. A savings goal aggregates contributions.\n4. Education cards explain the relevant concepts.\n5. Contextual insights connect the explanation to recorded numbers.\n6. The owner reviews and adjusts the next financial decision.\n\n## Validation\n\nFASE 8 must pass:\n\n- domain validation tests;\n- education service tests;\n- HTTP handler tests;\n- `go mod tidy`;\n- `go test ./...`;\n- `go build ./...`.
